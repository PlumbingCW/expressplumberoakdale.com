Terms of Service

Last updated November 16, 2026.

Using this site

The content here is general information about plumbing and our services. It is not a substitute for a visit from a plumber, and it does not create a contract by itself.

Service requests

Service is arranged by phone. A plumber describes the problem, the options and the price before work begins, and work starts once you agree. If the scope changes, we stop and discuss it before continuing.

Availability and arrival times depend on conditions such as weather, traffic and the number of urgent calls. We do not guarantee a specific arrival time.

Pricing and payment

Prices are given before work starts and are based on what the plumber can see at that time. Hidden conditions found during the work may change the scope, and we discuss any change with you first.

Safety information

Safety steps on this site, such as shutting off the main valve, are general guidance. If you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility. In a life threatening emergency, call 911.

Licensing

Check any California contractor license on the Contractors State License Board website before you hire. Permits and inspections are handled through your local building office where required.

Limits and changes

We make reasonable efforts to keep this site accurate, but we do not promise it is free of errors. We may change these terms, and the date above shows the latest update.
