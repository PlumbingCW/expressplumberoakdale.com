Emergency Burst Pipe Repair in Oakdale, CA

Water spraying from a wall, a ceiling that bulges or a lawn that turns into a pond means a pipe has failed. Shut the main valve, call and a plumber will be sent to stop the damage.

The first five minutes

  1. Shut off the main

    The main valve is usually at the house wall near the meter or where the line enters the garage. Turn it clockwise until it stops.

  2. Cut the power if water is near it

    If water reached outlets or the panel, switch off power at the breaker only if the path to the panel is dry.

  3. Open the lowest tap

    Running a faucet drains the pressure left in the lines and slows the spray.

  4. Move belongings

    Lift furniture, rugs and electronics out of the wet area.

  5. Call us

    Tell us where the water is and what pipe you can see, so the right parts are on the truck.

If you cannot find the main shutoff, call anyway. We can often guide you to it, or shut off at the meter box with a meter key.

Why pipes burst in the Central Valley

Freezing is the classic cause. The valley is mild, but the historic climate record near Oakdale shows about thirty nights a year at or below freezing, and a hard freeze can split exposed pipes in attics, garages and outdoor lines.

Pinholes from corrosion, a failed fitting, a water hammer shock and old galvanized steel that rusts from the inside cause just as many breaks. In older homes, a pipe that looks fine can have a paper thin wall, and one bump can open it.

What the repair involves

Pipe typeTypical failureRepair
CopperPinhole, freeze split or failed solder jointCut out the damage and sweat or press a new section
PEXSplit from freezing or a failed fittingReplace the section with crimp or expansion fittings
Galvanized steelRust through or a stripped threadReplace the run, as patching rarely lasts
CPVC or PVCCracked fitting or brittle pipeReplace the fitting and test nearby joints

We test the system after repair and check nearby sections, since a failure in one place often means weak pipe next to it. If most of the system is failing, see whole house repiping.

Water damage after the repair

Stopping the water is step one. Wet drywall, insulation and flooring need drying within a couple of days to avoid mold, and a restoration company can handle that while we handle the plumbing.

We document the break location and the failed part, which helps with an insurance claim. Take photos of damage before you clean up, and keep receipts for anything you buy to protect the home.

Pressure limits that protect your pipes

The Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), reflected in the California Plumbing Code, limits static water pressure inside a building to 80 psi and calls for a pressure reducing valve above that. Pressure that is too high is a quiet cause of burst pipes, so we test it after a repair.

Prevention that is worth doing

  • Know where the main shutoff is and make sure it turns easily.
  • Insulate exposed pipes in attics, garages and crawlspaces before winter.
  • Cover or drain outdoor hose bibs when a freeze is forecast.
  • Replace old flex connectors and corroded shutoff valves before they fail.

A pressure regulator that keeps pressure within a safe range also protects pipes and fixtures. The Burchell Hill area of Oakdale is served as a boosted pressure zone, so a gauge reading is worth taking. A plumber can check the regulator during a visit.
