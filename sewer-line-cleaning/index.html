Emergency Sewer Line Cleaning in Oakdale, CA

When the toilet, the tub and the floor drain all back up together, the main sewer line is blocked. That is a call that cannot wait, because the next flush can send sewage onto the floor.

We clear the main line, check the pipe afterward and tell you what caused the stoppage.

Stop the backup first

  • Stop using toilets, showers, sinks and the washing machine.
  • Keep people and pets away from any overflow.
  • Do not use chemical cleaners in a line that is already backing up.
  • Find the cleanout if you know where it is, but leave the cap in place until we arrive.

Sewage can carry bacteria, so protect your hands and clean only with proper gloves until a plumber takes over.

How we clear a main line

We open the cleanout, or the toilet flange when none exists, and feed a heavy duty cable machine into the line. The cutter bites through roots, paper and grease, and the line is flushed once flow returns.

Next we run a camera to see the pipe. A line that was blocked by one foreign object is a different problem from a line with root joints, and the camera lets us plan correctly. If the pipe wall is coated, hydro jetting gives a deeper cleaning.

If the camera shows a collapsed or offset section, sewer line repair is the real fix.

A cleanout makes everything cheaper

Many older Oakdale homes lack an accessible cleanout. Without one, access means pulling a toilet or going through the roof vent, which takes more time and risks the floor.

We can install a cleanout near the house, so the next visit takes minutes. It also lets a buyer inspector or a future plumber reach the line without disturbing your bathroom.

Why timing matters for a main line

A partial blockage often gives warnings first. Slow drains, gurgles after the washer runs and a faint sewer smell near a floor drain are all early signs. Calling at that stage usually means a simple clearing instead of an overflow and a floor to clean.

Water that has nowhere to go will find the lowest opening in the house. In many homes that is a shower drain or a basement floor drain, and the cleanup afterward is the larger bill. Early action costs less, so do not wait for the backup to reach the floor.

If a business line backs up

Shops and restaurants face the same stoppage with added pressure, since a closed restroom or kitchen loses revenue by the hour. We bring heavier machines for larger lines and plan the work around opening hours where possible. See commercial plumbing for the broader service.

Cleanouts and the plumbing code

The California Plumbing Code, based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), calls for cleanout access on a building sewer. If your main line cleaning meant pulling a toilet because no cleanout existed, adding one brings the line closer to what the code expects.

Run water for a few minutes and watch the lowest drains. If gurgling returns within days, call us back, because that can mean a partial blockage deeper in the line.
