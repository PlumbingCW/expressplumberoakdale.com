First steps when the water heater fails

  • If the tank is leaking from the body, close the cold water valve on top and switch off the gas or power.
  • If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility before you call us.
  • If the pilot is out on a gas unit, check the thermostat setting first, then call if it will not stay lit.
  • If the breaker for an electric unit keeps tripping, leave it off and call.

Do not drain or open anything on a tank that is making loud popping noises, because the sound can mean a thick layer of scale on the bottom of the tank.

A leak from a valve or a fitting is a repair. A leak from the tank itself is not, and the cost of patching it is better spent on a new heater.

Why scale and sediment matter in Oakdale

The city supplies groundwater from wells, and its published water quality data reported total hardness near 88 mg/L, which is moderately hard on the usual scale. That is not extreme, but over years the minerals settle on the tank floor and on electric elements.

Scale insulates the burner or the element from the water, so the unit works harder and fails sooner. A periodic flush and an anode rod check slow that process. The report we looked at is from 2013, so ask the city for its current water quality report if the exact number matters to you.

How the repair visit goes

We confirm the symptom, test the controls and check the venting or the electrical supply, then explain what failed. If a part is a few dollars and the tank is young, we fix it. If the tank is leaking or past its working life, we say so plainly and quote a replacement.

Safety checks come with the repair. We look at the temperature and pressure relief valve and its discharge pipe, the gas connector, the vent draft on gas units and earthquake strapping, since California homes are expected to have it.

Safety parts the code requires on every heater

The California Plumbing Code, built on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), requires a temperature and pressure relief valve with a discharge pipe that ends in a safe place. California Health and Safety Code section 19211 also calls for water heaters to be braced against earthquakes, so we check both on every repair visit.

Repair or replace, a plain answer

Tank heaters often last in the neighborhood of ten to fifteen years, depending on water and maintenance. A heater that is under that age with a failed part is worth fixing, while an older tank that has started to leak is not.

If you are weighing options, tankless water heaters avoid a tank to rust through, and planned installations can be scheduled for a time that works for you. We serve Riverbank and the surrounding towns from the same phone line, and newer neighborhoods such as Bridle Ridge have their own page.
