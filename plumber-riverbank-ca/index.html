Riverbank water and what it does to plumbing

The city serves its customers from nine active groundwater wells. Its published water quality data listed hardness averaging about 139 mg/L, with samples from roughly 62 to 216 mg/L, and sodium near 19 mg/L. That is hard water on the common scale, noticeably harder than what Oakdale reports.

For a homeowner that means faster scale in water heaters, crusty faucets and aerators that clog. A yearly flush of the tank and a scale filter or softener reduce the strain, and our water softener and filtration page explains the options. Check the city for its current report, since these numbers change by year.

Before We Arrive

  • Close the main shutoff valve if water is flowing and you cannot stop it.
  • Switch off the water heater if the tank is leaking.
  • Move rugs and electronics away from wet floors.
  • Take photos of any damage for your records.
  • Note the cleanout location if you know it.

These steps keep the damage small while you wait. If you are unsure where the main shutoff is, we can talk you through it on the phone.

Getting here and the shape of the town

Riverbank lies on the Stanislaus River, with Jacob Myers Park along the water and the BNSF rail line running through town. State Route 108 connects it to Oakdale, so the drive in from the east is a direct one.

The older core has homes with galvanized and mixed pipe, while newer areas lean on PEX and plastic drains. Salida, a short way toward Modesto, is also within our range, and callers there often start on this page.

Backflow and the city water division

The City of Riverbank runs a Cross Connection and Backflow Control Program through its Water Division, and the rules behind that kind of program come from California Code of Regulations Title 17 and the California Plumbing Code, based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). The division also lists (209) 869-7128 as an after hours emergency line for city water problems. If water is leaking on the street side of the meter, call the city line first.

Water that fails at the house is ours to help with. For a broken pipe between the meter and the house, see water line repair, and for a pipe burst inside, see burst pipe repair.

A plan for the next freeze or heat wave

Riverbank summers run hot and dry, and a few winter nights drop to freezing. Outdoor hose bibs, attic lines and pool plumbing take the most punishment, so we suggest checking them each fall and wrapping exposed lines.

Know where the main shutoff is and test it once a year. A valve that has not moved in a decade can seize, and finding that out in the middle of a flood is a bad time to learn it.
