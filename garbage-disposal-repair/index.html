Emergency Garbage Disposal Repair in Oakdale, CA

A disposal that hums but will not spin, a unit that leaks into the cabinet or a sink that fills with gray water usually has a jam, a worn seal or a clog just past the unit. Most can be fixed in one visit.

We free jams, replace leaking units and clear the drain line behind them, and we install new disposals when the old one is finished.

Safety first

  • Switch the disposal off at the wall switch and the breaker before you touch anything.
  • Never put your hand in the disposal, even when it is off.
  • Use tongs or pliers to remove a visible object.
  • Do not pour drain cleaner into a unit that is jammed or clogged.

If the unit is humming, it is trying to turn and cannot, which means a jam. Running it longer can burn out the motor.

A leak from the body of the unit cannot be repaired, because the housing is sealed and it is not worth a patch.

Clogs behind the disposal

A disposal does not clear the pipe after it. Grease, coffee grounds and fibrous scraps such as celery and corn husks stick in the trap and the drain line, and the sink fills even though the unit runs.

We clear the line with a cable and check the trap and the dishwasher connection. If the same clog returns, see drain cleaning for what a camera and a longer cable can find.

Replacing a disposal

Replacement makes sense when the motor has failed, the unit leaks or it is more than ten years old and noisy. We match the horsepower to your use, since a heavier unit grinds more with less strain.

  1. Disconnect

    We turn off the power and remove the old unit and the mounting assembly.

  2. Mount

    The new unit is fitted to the sink flange with a fresh seal.

  3. Connect

    The drain, the dishwasher inlet and the power are reconnected.

  4. Test

    We run water, check for leaks and show you how the reset works.

Choosing a replacement unit

Disposals range from light duty models for occasional use to heavy units with insulated chambers that run quieter. Households that cook daily or have large families gain from a stronger motor, while a small household can do well with a basic unit.

Features worth having include a stainless steel grinding chamber, a sound shield and a power cord or a hardwired option that matches your cabinet. We bring a couple of choices and install the one you pick, so you are not waiting on an order.

What the code says about a disposal drain

The California Plumbing Code, built on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), requires a disposal to drain through a trap, and a dishwasher connected to it needs backflow protection such as an air gap or a high loop as the code and the maker allow. We check the trap and the dishwasher line when we work on a disposal.

Habits that extend the life of a disposal

Run cold water while grinding and for a short time afterward, so fats move down the line before they harden. Skip grease, bones, pits, fibrous vegetables and large amounts of rice or pasta.
